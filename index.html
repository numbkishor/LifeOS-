Life OS

A personal, local-first life-management app for Windows and macOS.

Life OS keeps tasks, projects, plans, habits, money, notes and a journal in one place. It is built for a single person, on their own computer. It has no server, no user accounts, and no subscription.

Where your data lives

Everything is stored in one database file on your own computer. The app works fully offline.

Google, optionally

If you choose to connect your own Google account, Life OS can show your Google Calendar events on its planning grid. In a later version it will also be able to keep a backup copy of your database in your Google Drive's private app folder. Connecting Google is optional, and the app works the same without it.

Privacy policy

Life OS is a personal project and is not a commercial product or service.
