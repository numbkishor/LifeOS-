Privacy policy

Life OS · Effective 14 September 2026

Life OS is a desktop app for one person, running on that person's own computer. This policy explains what data it handles and where that data goes. In short: your data stays on your computer, and the only other place it ever goes is your own Google account, and only if you connect it.

Data stored on your computer

Everything you enter in Life OS is stored in a database file on your own computer: tasks, projects, plans, habits, money records, people, notes and journal entries. It stays there. The developer of Life OS does not operate any server, and has no way to see, collect or receive your data.

No analytics, tracking or advertising

Life OS contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind. Nothing is sent anywhere except to Google, and only as described below, after you connect your own account.

Google account (optional)

You can choose to connect your Google account. Life OS then talks directly between your computer and Google's servers. No other party is involved.

PermissionWhat Life OS does with it
View and edit events on your calendars
calendar.events		 Reads events from the calendars you select, to show them as busy time on the planning grid. In a later version, Life OS will also write your planned tasks to a separate calendar named "Life OS", which it creates. It never changes your other calendars' events.
See the list of your calendars
calendar.calendarlist.readonly		 Lists your calendars so you can choose which ones appear in Life OS.
Its own private app folder in Google Drive
drive.appdata (later version)		 Stores a backup copy of your Life OS database, so you can use it on your own second computer. This hidden folder can only be accessed by Life OS. Life OS cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Calendar events that Life OS reads are saved only in the database on your computer. Your Google sign-in is stored on your computer, encrypted by your operating system (Windows DPAPI or the macOS Keychain). It is never written to the database or sent anywhere except to Google.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Life OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the features described above, to you. It is not transferred to anyone, not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.

Disconnecting and deleting

  • Settings → Disconnect in Life OS revokes its access at Google, deletes the stored sign-in, and removes the calendar events it had copied.
  • You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account, under Third-party apps and services.
  • Deleting the Life OS database file from your computer deletes all of your Life OS data.

Children

Life OS is not directed at children under 13.

Changes
